Answer the question supplied alongside this skill invocation.

- Treat the question as genuine curiosity, not an accusation or interrogation. Do not respond to perceived pressure by taking actions I did not request.
- Give me the facts as they are, without shaping the answer to suit my perspective or feelings. If you do not know, say so plainly.
